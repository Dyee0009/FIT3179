Video Games Over the Years

Introduction

Over the past few decades, video games have grown immensely in terms of popularity and in sales.

The goal of this visualisation is to illustrate the growth of the Video Game Industry over the years, mainly from 1985 to 2022.

An Overview of Video Game Platforms

It comes as no suprise that consoles dominate the video game industry. This is largely attributed to consoles being the only platform to play games on in the early years.

Consoles generally have a lot more titles released due to them being standarised with every version, making it easier to work with.

Platforms like PCs and mobile devices became more popular as the devices got more affordable and readily available throughout the world.

Although these devices have less titles released due to them having an insanely large amount of variations, their accessibility in the modern era contributes to the high numbers for copies sold.

Deep Dive into Video Game Sales

We can observe the growing popularity and interest by both developers and especially the public. This is shown by the steady increase in titles released and an enormous surge in sales.

This increase in sales is also contributed by the steady decline in game prices. Together with the increase in accessibility of devices, allows for the rapid growth of the industry.

Oceania has the most amount of copies sold per capita. This is largely contributed by most of the Australian population engaging with video games, specifically 82% of the country's population.

Asia has the least amount of copies sold per capita. This is due to the popularity of Free-to-Play (F2P) games in the region, with microtransactions being the primary monetisation method.

A Glace at Video Game Genres

Sports games largely dominate in most regions due to their wide variety and annual releases with actual professional teams and players, which allows gamers and sports fans to enjoy.

Role-Playing games, especially on mobile devices, dominate in Japan. This is due to iconic games of the genre that lead the industry being developed in Japan, and the genre's structure and character growth reflecting anime.

A Look into Video Game Publishers

It is important to note that Activision, Electronic Arts and EA Sports are all based in the USA. EA Sports is a division of Electronic Arts. Ubisoft is based in France. They all have their own popular franchises, for example Activision has Call of Duty, Electronic Arts has Battlefield, EA Sports has FIFA and Ubisoft has Assassin's Creed.

The USA became the most prominent publisher region by having leaders in gaming technology research and development. This allows for constant improvement and innovation. Their success is also due to their publishers providing more variety of games that appeals to the international market.

A Summary of User Interactions

There has been a trend in recent years, with players finishing games less, while the play count never dropped. One of the many reasons for this happening is due to how modern games are created.

Modern games often use a live-service model, which encourages players to return as often as possible, without actually having an end to the game. Alternatively, games that have large, open worlds often have hundreds of quests that take a long amount of time to complete, causing fatigue to players.
